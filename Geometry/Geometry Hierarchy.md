# Geometry Hierarchy — seven levels, ordered by what structure is assumed

| # | Level | Primitive relation | Gloss |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Incidence** | *lies on* | points and lines only; no distance, no angle |
| 2 | **Affine** | *parallel* | parallelism without a metric ("straight-edge" geometry in the Confession) |
| 3 | **Ordered** | *is between* | order on a line |
| 4 | **Congruent** | *congruence* | same shape and size with no number attached |
| 5 | **Metric** | *distance* | numbers enter, but the geometry is abstract |
| 6 | **Euclidean / Hyperbolic / Spherical** | *perpendicular* | the parallel postulate selects the geometry |
| 7 | **Analytic** | *has coordinates* | algebra and geometry united |

**Rational Trigonometry** (Wildberger) — a reconstruction of trigonometry without irrational numbers: "should be taken seriously." Developed in this vault as [[Constructible Trigonometry]] (slope/length instead of quadrance/spread — a *different* but allied route; relation between the two is an open question, [[Future Directions]] item 9).

**Topology:** combinatorial topology is valid; "what topology has become" (point-set topology on ZFC) "has outrun the reality."

## Why the ordering matters for this project

Levels 1–4 need **no numbers at all** — so they carry no certificate burden and are the natural home of Euclid (the freshman year in [[Curriculum Plan]]). Number enters at level 5, and *which* numbers enter is the whole question of [[Number Hierarchy]]. Level 7 (coordinates) is where ℝ is usually smuggled in by Cartesian grid: "Descartes laid a grid over the world," after which "the Greeks' separation of arithmetic and geometry" was forgotten (WIM, ch. *Incommensurate*).

## Euclid's postulates, as the book presents them (WIM, ch. *Almost as Popular as Jesus*)

1. a straight line between any two points; 2. extended indefinitely; 3. a circle with any center and radius; 4. all right angles equal; 5. the parallel postulate. (Note that postulate 2 is *potential* extension — "indefinitely" — consistent with [[Creational Ultrafinitism]].)
