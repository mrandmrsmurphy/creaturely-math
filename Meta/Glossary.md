# Glossary

**Arithmetic / Mathematics / Metamathematics** — correctness / understanding / groundedness. [[Three Tiers]]

**Bamboozlement** — borrowing the certainty of an exact level and spending it at a weaker one under the same "="; collapsing levels 7–12 into ℝ "at no charge." [[Certificates]]

**Bargain** — every extension of number weakens "equals" and buys expressive power; the price is naming the certificate. [[Number Hierarchy]]

**Certificate** — the kind of warrant that entitles a mathematical "=" (exhibit, construct, converge, assign, or merely know-that-it-exists). [[Certificates]]

**Characteristic length ℓ** — $\ell=\pm\sqrt{1+m^2}$, signed by rightward (+) or leftward (−) orientation; the hypotenuse of the defining right triangle (base 1, height $m$); $=\sec\theta$. [[Characteristic Length]]

**Constructible field 𝕂** — numbers reachable from 0 and 1 by $+,-,\times,\div,\sqrt{}$. [[Constructible Numbers]]

**Creaturely realism** — objects are real and have natures; they are creational; finite minds reach them by construction. [[Creaturely Realism]]

**Debate Math** — mathematics as argument about what you may claim and on what warrant. [[Certificates]]

**Postures (five)** — attitudes toward mathematical truth, not eras: sapiential (receiving), Promethean (overreaching), unmasking (being exposed), repression (denial), chastened doxology (returning). [[Five Acts]]

**Doxological** — ending in praise rather than mastery; the Greek proof as worship; Q.E.D. as "a little eschatology." [[Theology of Number]], [[Five Acts]]

**Exhibitable / Geometric / Algorithmic / Twilight numbers** — the four certificate families of the hierarchy (levels 1–4 / 5–6 / 7–11 / 12–14). [[Number Hierarchy]]

**Falling power $n^{\underline k}$** — $n(n-1)\cdots(n-k+1)$; the basis adapted to Δ. **Δ** — forward difference. [[Discrete Calculus]]

**Formal identity (≡)** — equality of symbols under rules, with no claim to exhibition. [[Equal Signs]]

**Galois boundary** — the Abel–Ruffini wall: the general quintic is not solvable by radicals.

**Group law ⊕** — slope addition $m_{A+B}=\frac{m_A+m_B}{1-m_Am_B}$; equivalently the rational-circle group law. [[Constructible Trigonometry]], [[Discrete Calculus]]

**ISS** — Inradius-Side-Side: a newly identified ambiguous triangle case that reduces to cubics. [[Constructible Trigonometry]]

**Jiǔ Zhāng numbers 𝕁** — $n$-th roots with a determinate digit algorithm, after the *Nine Chapters* (ch. 4, *Shaoguang*). Level 7.

**Quadriga Scientiae** — Murphy's four arts of symbolic knowledge, a Protestant (and, in a draft, ecumenical) extension of Pólya; the Book-of-Nature counterpart of the medieval fourfold sense of Scripture. [[Quadriga for Mathematics]]

**Quaestio / Noesis / Praxis / Apodeixis** — question-posing / insight / faithful execution / demonstration by necessity: the four arts. [[Quaestio]], [[Noesis]], [[Praxis]], [[Apodeixis]]

**Word / Symbol** — the Trivium masters the *word* (rich, ambiguous, personal); the Quadriga masters the *symbol* (stripped of richness so it can be handled mechanically), always under the *Logos*. [[Quadriga for Mathematics]]

**Four kinds of truth** — revelation; properly basic; logical; verisimilitude (observations are data for the last). [[Four Kinds of Truth]]

**Potential vs. actual infinity** — a process that never ends (accepted) vs. a completed totality (refused). Aristotle, *Physics* III.4–8. [[Creational Ultrafinitism]]

**Seqt** — as the textbook outline calls it, the pyramid-face slope measure of Egyptian/Babylonian building practice (Egyptian *seked*); an ancient instance of "slope as the primitive." [[Constructible Trigonometry]]

**Slope $m$** — rise over run, $\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}$; the slope of the wedge at a vertex is $m_A$. The primitive of constructible trigonometry. [[Slope]]

**Creational ultrafinitism** (earlier name: tempered ultrafinitism) — potential infinity yes; actual infinity no; very large finite numbers: graduated skepticism ("notation, not number"). [[Creational Ultrafinitism]]

**Three equal signs** — exact, algorithmic, formal. [[Equal Signs]]

**Twelve Exercises** — Analogy, Generalization, Specialization, Break it up, Think backwards, Add to the problem, Logical extremes, Induction, Conjecture, Symmetry, Reframing, Rest: the virtues of Noesis, each with a Latin pair. [[Twelve Exercises]]

**Triperspectivalism** — Frame/Poythress: normative, situational, existential; applied as orthodoxy / orthopraxy / orthopathy. [[Theology of Number]]

**WIM** — the working title/file of the *What is Mathematics* book: *Why Non-Christian Math Stinks*. [[Curriculum Plan]]
