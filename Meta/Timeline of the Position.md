## What changed, what didn't

- **Constant since 2020:** realism about mathematical objects; theological grounding; Kline; classical-education method; Wildberger as ally; skepticism about the ontology of ℝ-style completion.
- **Sharpened:** from Cantor's institutionalizations and Gödel as cataclysm (2020) → Gödel as *confirmation* and the diagonal as a *boundary marker* (2026). From "Robinson's infinitesimals" (2020) → ε²=0 (2023) → discrete calculus (2026), all read as infinitesimals = reciprocals of unmanageable numbers ([[Infinitesimals]]).
- **New in 2026:** the **certificate** vocabulary; the **14-level hierarchy**; the physical-bound arithmetic for the ultrafinitism; the **five-act** schema.
- **Not yet reconciled:** logic home ([[Structure Hierarchies]]); the 3-sign vs 14-level equality taxonomies ([[Equal Signs]]).
