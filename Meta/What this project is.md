# What this project is

*Mathematics as a finite creature does it, before an infinite God.*

This vault works out one conviction: **we are creatures, and God alone is infinite.** God ordered all things "in measure, number, and weight" (Wisdom 11:20), so that order of mathematics is real — discovered, not invented. Our order is *created*, not self-existent, because we don't think God's thought as He does. And the minds that study it are finite. The whole project is what mathematics looks like when those three things are held together and taken seriously at every step.

## The position in three parts

**What numbers are — [[Creaturely Realism]].** Mathematical objects have natures we *discover* (against the view that they are labels or fictions); they belong to the creation, not to a self-existent Platonic realm; and finite minds know them by construction and approach, never by direct apprehension of an infinite whole. God knows $\pi$ completely, every digit, without convergence; we approach it by an algorithm that never arrives. That partiality is not a defect — it is what creaturely knowledge of a created object looks like.

**How we know them — certificate-explicit constructivism ([[Certificates]]).** Every "=" is a *claim*, and every claim needs an exhibited warrant — a certificate, which is a procedure. Before any equals sign, the question is: *what is your manner of proof?* You can exhibit the thing (a count), construct it (compass and straightedge), converge to it (an algorithm with a stated error bound), or assign it by a naming convention — and each of those is a different kind of "=".

**How big mathematics is — [[Creational Ultrafinitism]].** Potential infinity, yes: every $n$ has an $n+1$. Completed infinity, no: the Axiom of Infinity is a theological claim disguised as a mathematical axiom. And past a threshold you are holding *notation, not a number* — the skepticism is graduated, a rising cost rather than a cliff, and the bound is the creation's own (about $10^{140}$ physical events in the observable universe).

## The word "number," split

The word *number* today is like the word *star* two thousand years ago: gas giants, rocky planets, stars and galaxies all went by one name. The vault sorts them: **Numbers** (levels 1–4, decidable by a finite check), **Lengths** (5–6, exact arithmetic with roots), **Pixels** (7–11, equality means agreement at a stated resolution), **Posits** (12–14, equality by a stated convention). See [[Number Hierarchy]] and [[Numbers, Lengths and Pixels]].

## What the vault has done with this

- A fourteen-level [[Number Hierarchy]] and its application to functions ([[Function Hierarchy]]); Cantor's diagonal read as a procedural negative about lists ([[Diagonal Argument]]).
- A trigonometry with no angle measure and no transcendentals: fifteen triangle cases plus the area, inradius and circumradius cases ([[Constructible Trigonometry]]), and a discrete rational orbit in place of sine and cosine ([[Rational Rotation]]).
- A first-order calculus with $\varepsilon^2=0$ ([[Calculus]], [[Infinitesimals]]), with the fundamental, intermediate value, extreme value, mean value and Bolzano–Weierstrass theorems each worked in budget form.
- Statistics begun finitely, with explicit error bounds ([[Constructible Statistics]]); finite fields, groups and symmetries ([[Groups]]).
- A pedagogy for all of it ([[Quadriga for Mathematics]]).

## What is not being claimed

- **Classical mathematics is not false, as a game played with its rules.** Its results largely stand; bridges hold. The vault's question is different: what may a *creature* assert, and on what warrant? Standard analysis collapses several different kinds of "=" into one object, ℝ, and charges nothing for the move; the project is to name the cost, not to deny the game.
- **This is not a known school.** It is not Platonism, formalism, intuitionism, or "just" constructivism — it is the three-part position above, held for theological reasons. Brouwer's constructivism is the closest neighbor in method, but it grounds construction in human intuition; here the objects are real before we construct them, and construction is our *access*, not their cause.

Which claims are settled and which are open is not a matter of tone: the Settled table in the Agenda ([[Future Directions]]) is the whole list of what is settled, and everything else on the Agenda is open work.
