# My Math

*Mathematics as a finite creature does it, before an infinite God.*

> **Creaturely realism** (what numbers are) · **certificate-explicit constructivism** (how we know them) · **creational ultrafinitism** (how big mathematics is).
> Every "=" needs a stated warrant. Potential infinity yes; completed infinity no; past a threshold, a notation is not a number. The bound is the creation's: we are creatures, and God alone is infinite.
>
> The word *number* today is like the word *star* two thousand years ago. The vault sorts the planets from the stars from the galaxies — Numbers, Lengths, Pixels ([[Number Hierarchy]], [[Numbers, Lengths and Pixels]]).
