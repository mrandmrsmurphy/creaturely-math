## The *what is mathematics* book

***Why Non-Christian Math Stinks*** (WIM). A popular book with a deliberate arc:

- **Part I, The Fall** — drafted: *You're a Survivor* (the "I hated math" encounter); *No Way José* (arithmetic; **counting and multiplying in Babylonian**; the divisibility of 60; 1/7); *Incommensurate* (Greeks; Hippasus; √2); *Almost as Popular as Jesus* (Euclid; the five postulates). **Stubs:** *To Indeed Be a god* (zero, negatives, imaginaries); *Get Off Your High Horse and Measure* (scientific revolution); *Maybe We are God…* (calculus, exaltation of Newton); *Falling Like Lightning* (multiple geometries, loss of certainty); *Making Our Own Universe* (Cantor, Dedekind, Cauchy, Hilbert on infinity and the reals).
- **Part II, Christian Worldview** — *all four chapters empty:* **Fallen and Finite · Creaturely Realism · Certificate Constructivism · Biblical Ultra-finitism.** These are exactly [[Creaturely Realism]], [[Certificates]], [[Creational Ultrafinitism]] — *this vault is Part II's raw material.*
- **Part III, Walking in Math** — empty: [[Quaestio]] · [[Noesis]] · [[Praxis]] · [[Apodeixis]] ([[Quadriga for Mathematics]]). Plus a Book list.
- It is organized around [[Five Acts]] / the 2020 redemptive scheme.

## Pedagogical commitments appearing in every source

- **Historical order** (Confession; article; Kline) — [[Counting and Bases]]; developed on [[Historical Order]] (including the two tracks at the end, advanced and [[Statistics]]).
- **Problems-based instruction** — "the only way that preserves the dignity of the student" (2020 essay); plus Exeter/AoPS.
- **Trivium for math** — grammar (times tables, formulas), logic (conjectures, "is an odd number to an odd power necessarily odd?"), **rhetoric** (talks, debates; "a final exam might be a debate on the existence or necessity of infinitesimals"). "Mathematics practice in this country is stuck at the grammar stage."
- **Breadth over a death march to calculus** — modular arithmetic in elementary school; geometry beyond ninth grade (inversion); number theory; topology.
- **Math as a liberal art** — quadrivium grid (Caldecott): Arithmetic (number) · Geometry (+space) · Music (+time) · Astronomy (+space +time); e.g., the perfect fifth 2:3 vs. its equal-tempered $2^{7/12}\approx1.498$.
- **Against the pendulum** — NCTM's five strands; [[Corrective Narrative]]; the Classical Baccalaureate Math Committee (Ravi Jain, 2026-08-10) is a parallel, less foundational effort.
