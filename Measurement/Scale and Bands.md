# Scale and Bands

Not "math" strictly — but it states in physics the *same* principle [[Creational Ultrafinitism]] states in number: **continuity of notation is not continuity of nature.**

| Band | Scale (m) | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Trivium | logically prior | words, meaning — "the conditions for any knowledge whatsoever"; *no* scale |
| Cosmological | $10^{25}$–$10^{18}$ | "least empirically grounded, most assumption-laden" |
| Galactic | $10^{18}$–$10^{11}$ | inherits cosmological assumptions |
| Orbital | $10^{11}$–$10^{4}$ | "most epistemically secure band we have" |
| Terrestrial | $10^{4}$–$10^{-3}$ | "our native band"; observer and observed at the same scale |
| Chemistry | $10^{-3}$–$10^{-10}$ | reductionism most tempting and most incomplete |
| Quantum | $10^{-10}$–$10^{-17}$ | "most precisely verified science within its domain" |

## Principles

- **Band-gap principle:** adjacent bands overlap; bands two steps apart need not. Knowledge is **modular, not hierarchical**: "the chain [of reduction] is broken at every other link." "A feature of Creation": a hierarchy of *logoi*.
- **Quantum gravity as category error:** unifying $10^{-17}$ m with $10^{25}$ m asks one theory to span many non-adjacent bands. The error is **"the idolatry of symbolic structure"** — *"we can write a symbol that spans all scales; therefore, we assume, there must be a reality that corresponds to it… the confusion of the map with the territory, and worse, the confusion of the map-maker's power with the Creator's."*
- **The Theory of Everything as Babel;** Pentecost = many tongues, one truth; "we are attempting to solve the One-and-Many with the One being Us."
- **Quadriga sciences:** band-bounded; praxis-centered; multi-generational; *semper reformata*; anti-reductionist.
- **Aseity:** God "gained nothing by making the universe"; we measure *because we do not know*: "the proper glory of the creature." Cf. [[Creaturely Realism]].

## The creature in the middle

God has put humans in a universe both bigger and smaller than they are, and slower and faster. Without tools we cannot observe what takes hundreds of years or a fraction of a millisecond, what is larger than our field of vision, or what is smaller than about a hair's width. The native band is a *place*, not a viewpoint from which everything is visible.

Mathematics, with instruments, does make the invisible visible: it lets a creature reach the very large, the very small, the very slow and the very fast. But the extension is *by means* (a lens, a clock, a ruler, a calculation, each with a cost and a tolerance), and it is not a passport out of creaturehood. Reach is not omniscience: the further from the native band, the more assumption-laden the result (the table's own notes on the cosmological and chemical bands), and the symbol that spans every scale is not thereby a window onto every scale. This is the same thing as the arithmetic of [[Creational Ultrafinitism]]: the tools extend us, and the budget remains. (Murphy.)

Three consequences for the vault's mathematics:

- **A measured quantity is a number with a tolerance** from a tool that has a range ([[Number in Natural Philosophy]]); an exact rational for a length is a model, not an observation ([[Four Kinds of Truth]]: observations are data for verisimilitudes).
- **Every scale has its own numerals.** The digits that mean something at $10^{-3}$ m mean nothing at $10^{-17}$ m, and the ratio between scales is itself a finite, nameable number, not a licence to carry the same notation across the gap ([[Exact Until Output]]).
- **Making the invisible visible is a vocation, and a limited one** ([[Praxis]]: *Mensura*, to proportion confidence to what the execution warrants; [[Quaestio]]: is this a question we can put to a tool at this scale at all?).

## Why it matters for the math *(AI gloss — not in Murphy's text)*

ℝ is the mathematical version of "one equation for everything": a notation that spans all scales (it will happily give a meaningful 'distance' of $10^{-1000}$ m) and so is presumed to correspond to a continuum of nature. The ultrafinitist reply: *Quaestio first* — "is this a question that admits of symbolic treatment, or have we mistaken the continuity of our notation for the continuity of nature?" See also [[Number in Natural Philosophy]].
