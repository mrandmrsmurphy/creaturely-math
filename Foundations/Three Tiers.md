# Three Tiers: arithmetic, mathematics, metamathematics

> **Arithmetic** — the computation of calculable values by exact algorithms within a stated system. Success = *correctness* (it works or it doesn't).
> **Mathematics** — the human study of number, shape, structure, and change, together with the tools developed in that study. Success = *understanding*: not just *that* but *why*, and *what kind of thing*.
> **Metamathematics** — the study of mathematics itself: foundations, limits, tools, nature. "Mathematics becoming self-aware." Success = *groundedness*: *what the system is* and *what we are entitled to assume*.

Parallel in CS: running a program is arithmetic; studying how to make and understand programs is CS proper. Most school "math" is arithmetic. In the [[Quadriga for Mathematics]] these map roughly to *Praxis* (arithmetic) and *Quaestio/Noesis/Apodeixis* (mathematics), with metamathematics as the discipline of keeping *Quaestio* honest. ([[Quaestio]], [[Praxis]], [[Quadriga and the Position]].)

## How the definition evolved (merged here to avoid three competing definitions)

| Date | Wording | Note |
|---|---|---|
| c.2020 | Two-fold: (1) a **method** — quantitative reasoning; (2) a **science** — reasoning about abstract objects "beginning with quantity itself," borrowing Devlin's "science of patterns… *and of the mathematical tools used in this study*" | Distinction "clears up the perennial debate of the relationship between mathematics and logic." Also: "math is the language of **things**; English… of **persons**." |
| 2023 (Confession) | "The science of numbers, shape, change, and the mathematical tools themselves… the process of abstraction itself." Poincaré: *"the art of giving the same name to different things."* | "'Equal' in mathematics is not like 'equal' in normal speech." |
| WIM draft | "A spectrum, from calculation to a certain kind of philosophy" | Arithmetic is the calculation edge |
| 2026 | The three-tier distinction above | The latest and most developed |

**Reconciliation:** 2026's *mathematics* = 2020's *science* (narrow sense); 2020's *method* (broad sense) survives as the claim that quantitative reasoning is a general mode of inquiry — which the [[Quadriga for Mathematics]] guards with *Quaestio* ("is this a mathematical question at all?").

The Confession's "Poincaré" line is the seed of [[Certificates]]: if mathematics is giving one name to different things, the discipline is saying *which* things, and *on what warrant*.
