# Theology of Number

The Scripture-and-theology layer under the whole project. Most of it comes from the 2020 essay; the 2026 material (five acts, doxology) is the same story told with new vocabulary — see [[Timeline of the Position]].

See also [[Theological Grounds]] for a commitment-by-commitment map of texts, strength and objections.

## One and Three — a numerical paradox

God is One (Dt 6:4) and Three (Mt 28:19). "We possess knowledge analogical to God's own idea of Oneness, and knowledge analogical to his idea of Threeness, but we are incapable of reconciling the two" — a *paradox, not a contradiction*. So mathematics begins in **epistemological humility**. (Cf. one-and-many in the wider wiki.)

- Genesis 1:27 oscillates between singular and plural humanity; Gen 2:24's "binity" (two become one flesh). Every language has grammatical number.
- Numbering reveals comprehensibility; **innumerability** reveals incomprehensibility: miracles "without number" (Job 5:9), understanding "without measure" (Ps 147:5), no beginning or end (Ps 90:2), "infinite, eternal, and unchangeable" (Shorter Catechism).
- Axioms are "statements about the character of God." Abstraction and instantiation belong together (Poythress: "equal ultimacy of the one and the many").
- Natural laws are "**God's customary ways of acting**" (Chesterton) — he does not tire of repetition as we do; mathematical laws are "descriptions of the internal coherence and character of God." Guard against the *Force in Star Wars* picture.

## Redemptive-historical frame (2020)

| Phase | Mathematical form |
|---|---|
| **Creation** | The ordered, numbered creation; dominion mandate ("be fruitful and multiply"); metaphorical language of creation (Jordan, *Through New Eyes*) |
| **Rebellion** | The One/Many split. **Many-ness wins** in B.C. empiricism (Babylon, Egypt: receipts, procedures, "S.T.E.M." as the lay view). **One-ness wins** in rationalism (the Grand Unified Theory; "everything simplifiable to one equation"; Greek deduction). Kline's *Loss of Certainty*; Cantor, Gödel, Cohen; schools that "dare not engage each other" |
| **Redemption** | Triperspectival: **orthodoxy / orthopraxy / orthopathy** (Frame, Poythress: normative / situational / existential). Method = classical education (trivium → quadrivium) |
| **Consummation** | A reconstructed classroom: laws as God's ways; math as art (Euler's $e^{i\pi}+1=0$ as Rembrandt); problems-based instruction because "the dignity of the student" requires wrestling; love; breadth beyond the "death march to calculus"; "every good thing will be carried forward into eternity" |

This **four-phase** frame is re-expressed as the five **postures** of [[Five Acts]] (Sapiential, Promethean, Unmasking, Repression, Chastened Doxology), ordered by logic and not by date. The Rebellion corresponds to the second to fourth postures. For the creaturely realist, the exposure of the overreaching programme is "confirmation, not catastrophe".

A charity toward Babylon (Murphy): technically all pagan mathematics is rebellion, since it does not follow Christ, so Babylon stands in that row. But its many-ness is to be called **a far side of the pagan spectrum, not a driving over the cliff** — an honest engineering empiricism ([[Babylonian Mathematics]]), not the defiant overreach of the later rationalism.

## Numbers in Scripture

- **John 6** (feeding of the five thousand): Philip answers with *forecasts* (cost); Andrew with an *inventory* (5 loaves, 2 fish); Jesus gives thanks; then the *leftovers are counted* (12 baskets). Numbers return "only with the meaning they are given by God and his word." Twelve is symbolic (Jordan). Further examples: triangular numbers in Jn 21:11; Acts 27:37. (Murphy's 2020 *What is Mathematics* draft.)
- **Wisdom 11:20** — "measure, number, and weight." Augustine, *De lib. arb.* II.8 (the numbers by which we reckon are not our invention but divine illumination).
- **Buried treasure** (Prov 25:2; Gen 2:10–14): "the world is basically one big Easter egg hunt"; some treasures are gold and bdellium, others the Pythagorean Theorem or Fermat's Last Theorem — "buried treasure between our ears."

## Three values and the mean

The balanced digits $+1,0,-1$ ([[Balanced Ternary]]) and the values of [[Three-Valued Logic]] can be read as three acts of reason: **affirmation** ($+1$, true), **suspension** ($0$, unknown), **denial** ($-1$, false). Binary logic has only affirmation and denial; the third restores the *mean* that holds them in tension. Murphy's further readings:

- **Metaphysical:** actuality $\leftrightarrow+1$, potential $\leftrightarrow0$, privation $\leftrightarrow-1$.
- **Mediation:** $1+(-1)=0$ is not only arithmetic; contraries resolve in a mean. The Mediator unites opposites (divinity and humanity, justice and mercy) "without confusion or division", and the middle digit $0$ reconciles $+1$ and $-1$. The booklet calls this **an analogy, not an identification**: the mean is a structural echo of mediation.
- **Humility and prudence:** the middle value gives formal structure to prudence, the virtue that governs assertion and denial. "Humility does not deny truth; it honors it by waiting."
- **Number and logic as one order:** "To think truly is to count rightly"; logic is the formal cause of reasoning and arithmetic its quantitative matter.

Two difficulties, flagged and not smoothed over. (1) **Privation as $-1$, and the equality of $+$ and $-$.** On the Thomistic account a privation is the absence of a due good, not a negative being; reading it as the opposite of actuality on a number line risks treating evil as a counter-substance. Murphy adds a second consideration from physics. Wildberger makes a great deal of negative numbers being just as important as positive ones, grounding it in the physicists' antiparticles. But matter vastly outnumbers antimatter in the universe, and science has no answer yet as to why. So treating the two signs as exact equals is also theologically suspect: the creation is not symmetric in the way the number line is. Balanced ternary's symmetry is a fact about the digits, and should not be read as a fact about the world ([[Balanced Ternary]]). (2) **The two roles of $0$.** In logic $0$ means *unknown*, which is about the knower; in the metaphysical reading $0$ means *potential*, which is about the thing. These are different, and the booklet's table puts them together.

## Babel and doxology

Standard analysis treating ℝ as a number system that swallows all certificates = mathematical Babel (Gen 11:4). The Greek proof begins as **doxology** — the legend of the sacrifice of an ox (or oxen) for a geometric discovery ("almost certainly apocryphal… theologically accurate"). Q.E.D. is "a little eschatology." See [[Certificates]], [[Five Acts]].

## Math is the language of *things*

The quadrivium assumes quantitative reasoning; "the fatuous assumption that everything can be quantified" is refuted by the creation account itself. "Math is the language of **things**. English… the language of **persons**." Logos precedes number: *"In the beginning was the Word,"* not the algorithm — the root of *Quaestio* in [[Quadriga for Mathematics]].
