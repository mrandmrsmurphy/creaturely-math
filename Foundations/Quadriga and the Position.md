# The Quadriga and the three-part position

The Quadriga was developed as a separate project: a way to teach any symbolic inquiry, built from Pólya and a Protestant (and, in the draft for Catholic readers, ecumenical) account of work and knowledge. This vault is the first attempt to hold all of Murphy's mathematics in one place. This page is where the two are joined. It says what each project is *about* so that the join is clear.

> The position says **what mathematics is** (creaturely realism), **how we know it** (certificate-explicit constructivism), **how big it is** (creational ultrafinitism).
> The Quadriga says **how a creature does and teaches it**: pose the question, see the structure, do the work, show the result.

They have met in Murphy's own hands once. His slides for a Friday talk in June 2026, *Mathematics as a Liberal Art: Debate Math, Not Computation*, open with Pólya and the four arts, then went through the number hierarchy and named $\mathbb R$ "math's Tower of Babel", exactly as the Scale framework calls the Theory of Everything science's. Its own formulation of the join: "Debate Math" is the habit of asking, before writing any "=", *what license do I have to write this?* and "proof is just the right certificate for the level you're claiming."

## The eight marks, and what each says about mathematics

The Quadriga rests on eight claims it calls marks (the Protestant statement is canonical here; the draft for Catholic readers re-grounds the same marks on the Church's shared teaching, and renames *Semper Reformata* "Development"). Each touches a part of the position.

| Mark | The claim (Murphy) | Where it lands in the vault |
|---|---|---|
| **Catholicity** | The practical was always inside the liberal arts; the contempt for it was aspirational snobbery. | [[Mathematics 500 to 1500]] (the Romans; applied mathematics counted), [[Babylonian Mathematics]] |
| **Tradition** | The actual habits were times tables, Pythagorean triples, constructions, not contemplating polygonal numbers. | [[Historical Order]], [[Tally Marks]], [[Constructible Trigonometry]] |
| **Specificity** | The aim is the dignity of operative knowing, no more. | the vault's refusal to overreach: potential infinity yes, completed no |
| **Scriptural** | The Bible is quantitative (Tabernacle, census, genealogies). | [[Theology of Number]], [[Number in Natural Philosophy]] |
| **Christocentric** | Jesus the *tekton*: to denigrate technical knowing is to denigrate the humanity he assumed. | [[Praxis]]; proof by construction in [[Proof Types]] |
| **Anthropological** | *Imago Dei*: we know by engaging "the resistant particularity of creation", not by contemplation alone. | [[Creaturely Realism]]: structures are there to be perceived and labored at |
| **Hamartological** | Total depravity yields community: "I am not trustworthy alone". | [[Certificates]] (checkable by others); the ethical form in [[Christians and the Reals]]; [[Four Kinds of Truth]] |
| **Semper Reformata** | Every theory provisional; the scientist with the "final theory" is the Restorationist of knowledge. | the vault's own rule to fix and flag; the posture typology of [[Five Acts]] |

## What each vice names in the vault

The Latin vices of the four arts are a diagnostic vocabulary for the problems the vault has already identified in standard mathematics.

| Vice | The standard-mathematics form | Page |
|---|---|---|
| *Negligentia*, *Indeterminatio* | Never asking "which equality?"; leaving *number* undefined so that a notation passes for a number | [[Equal Signs]], [[Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic]] |
| *Confusio* | Levels 7 to 12 collapsed into one object, $\mathbb R$; the kinds of number sold as one | [[Number Hierarchy]], [[Numbers, Lengths and Pixels]] |
| *Praesumptio* | Assuming a completed continuum, or a completed $\mathbb N$, at the outset; continuity with no modulus | [[Intermediate Value Theorem]], [[Christians and the Reals]] |
| *Superstitio* | Trusting a procedure or a stored decimal without knowing why it works; "it works, so use it" | [[Exact Until Output]], [[Corrective Narrative]] |
| *Mechanismus* | The checked proof as the end; mathematics as symbol-pushing | [[Mathematics 1900 to Today]] |
| *Persuasio*, *Obscuritas* | The "$-\tfrac1{12}$" video; "it's a real number"; notation that feels like an object | [[Apodeixis]], [[Exhibiting a Real]] |
| *Festinatio*, *Occultatio* | Publishing before review; proofs that only the author or a machine can check | [[Mathematics 1900 to Today]] |

## What each project gives the other

**To the position, the Quadriga gives a way of teaching what it has only argued.** The vault is rich in theses and thin in classroom method. The four arts name the order of a lesson, the Twelve Exercises name what to practise, the failure modes name what to diagnose, and the proof types (a plan, not yet a year of teaching) name what to ask for in writing. It also supplies a **standing discipline for the philosophy**: *Quaestio* is not done once at the start, and "which equality?" is a question to be asked again at each upward move of the hierarchy.

**To the Quadriga, the position gives an account of what *Apodeixis* is a proof *of*.** The source says a proof shows "a heretofore unknown aspect of reality" (realism) and demands necessity (constructivism); it does not say what a creature may count as necessary, or how big a demonstration can be. The certificate vocabulary and the finite budget answer those. It also gives the Quadriga a **concrete example of a Persuasio failure** that is mathematical and not a lab report: the story of $\mathbb R$.

## Tensions and open questions

1. **"Not outsourceable."** The Quadriga says insight (Rest, Symmetry) cannot be done by software; the vault records machine-generated mathematics. See [[Noesis]], hard problem 1.
2. **Necessity over infinitely many cases.** *Necessitas* needs a reading under potential infinity. See [[Apodeixis]], hard problem 1.
3. **Repeatability and the budget.** What *Imitabilitas* demands of a proof past the threshold. See [[Apodeixis]].
4. **The extent of "symbol".** The source says the Quadrivium masters the *symbol*: mathematics, logic, musical counterpoint, even computer science. The vault is about number, shape, structure and change. Whether the vault's [[Three Tiers]] and the Quadriga's "symbolic arts" are meant to draw the same boundary is not said.
5. **Number and notation.** The source says the unifying medium is not "number" but formal symbol. The vault's central warning, "a notation is not a number", is a warning that *symbol* and *number* come apart. The two ideas look to be in agreement, and the Quadriga's *Quaestio* is where they meet.
