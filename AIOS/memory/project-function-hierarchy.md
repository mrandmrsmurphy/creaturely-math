Page: `Number/Function Hierarchy`. Murphy's points: levels 1-4 are combinations of arithmetic (ℚ→ℚ, exact), 5 adds √, 6 adds ∛, etc.; a polynomial is an exact function and its Pixel nature appears only when one solves; a transcendental is a limit (level 10 or 11 number) for every input; two axes: certificate of the operations, and computational cost to a given pixel range; Pixel inputs are not worth considering in rating the function (everyone rounds the input first).

Also on the page: level 14 (analytic continuation, ζ(-1) = -1/12) and a differential-equations section (Euler is exact ℚ→ℚ; certificate = method's error theorem; cost; chaos horizon).

Open: cost theorems (big-O) are not proved; several variables; whether level 9 (geometric definitions) is its own level for functions; the exp/log/trig pages under `Calculus` are still unwritten and would hang from this page.
