Murphy was irritated at being repeatedly prompted for a sine and cosine: in his hierarchy they are transcendental functions, level 10 or 11 ([[project-function-hierarchy]]).

**Why:** the vault's stance is that a limit-defined value is the far end of trust; the vault's trigonometry runs on slopes, characteristic lengths and exact constructible values.

**How to apply:** never propose "define sin and cos" as a next step or ask where they are. When a circular function is needed, use `Trigonometry/Rational Rotation` (the tick sine and cosine, exact, levels 1-4) or the slope addition law. Name wedges concretely, no angle measure in `Trigonometry/`. See [[project-rational-rotation]].
