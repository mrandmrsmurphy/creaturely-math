The Quadriga Scientiae (Quaestio, Noesis, Praxis, Apodeixis) is Murphy's separate project, a Protestant extension of Pólya for teaching symbolic work. Documented 2026-10-10 from `Sources/Quadriga Scientiae - Complete.md`, a compilation of his manuscripts (the canonical originals stay outside the vault: `raw/murphy/Quadriga Scientiae.md`, `raw/Drafts/Quadriga Proposal.md`, `wiki/pedagogy/quadriga-scientiae.md`). The first export was cut off partway through Part IV and was re-uploaded complete.

Vault pages: `Pedagogy/{Quadriga for Mathematics, Quaestio, Noesis, Praxis, Apodeixis, Twelve Exercises}`, `Foundations/{Four Kinds of Truth, Quadriga and the Position}`, `Pedagogy/Proof Types` (Quadriga column), `Measurement/Scale and Bands` ("The creature in the middle").

**Why it matters:** it is Murphy's answer to how to teach; the position is his answer to what mathematics is. The join is the certificate: proof (Apodeixis) is the right certificate for the level claimed.

**Open for Murphy (all in `Log/log.md`, 2026-10-10):** "Luther's quadriga" (Luther favoured the literal sense); *Religiosa* vs *Religionis*; the Appendix glosses of Specialization and Adjunctio differ from the body; Appendix names one vice per exercise while the body warns of the opposite excess for Symmetry and Conjecture; the five Quaestio offices pair with the Latin only by position; "software cannot practise Rest or Symmetry" vs machine-generated mathematics; whether *Imitabilitas* should be read under a finite budget (an [AI] proposal); the ecumenical draft (for Catholic readers; first reader Dan Murphy of Western Academy, Houston) is unreviewed.
