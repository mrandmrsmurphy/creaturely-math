When asked what exp, ln, sin, cos are "for", I offered "names" as a role. Murphy rejected it: new antiderivative functions (Si, Li) are custom names; the better convention is to say what part is rational, what a Length, what only a Pixel. Example: the antiderivative of 1+1/(x²+1) is x (clear) plus arctan x (only approximatable, series).

**Why:** his system's one change from modern math is pointing out exact versus approximate, always.

**How to apply:** when presenting any answer containing a transcendental, split it into parts and label each with its level and certificate (see `Number/Function Hierarchy`, "Reading an answer by parts"). Do not defend functions by their names or treat them as needed. State the range of any series certificate (arctan: |x|≤1). Related: [[feedback-no-sine-cosine]], [[project-function-hierarchy]].
