Murphy moved away from keeping his previous sources: the vault had advanced past them, and they were weights holding back new writing, not authorities to preserve.

**Why:** canonical pages supersede the drafts; fidelity to a superseded draft blocks new work.

**How to apply:** where a draft and a canonical page differ, the page governs; prose attribution ("Murphy, 2023") suffices for quotations — no file needs to stay for a quotation's sake ([[feedback-sources-citations]]). **Done 2026-11-07: the whole `Sources/` folder is deleted.** The Quadriga manuscripts became canonical pages ([[project-quadriga]]); the Catechism, Confession, c2020 essay, WIM draft, me.md section, raw 2026-05 draft, CT textbook and both DC drafts were audited, absorbed where needed, and deleted. Recorded losses (recoverable from git history): the Quadriga compilation's Parts IV–V (coverage index, development chronicle) and the CT textbook's section-level outline detail.
