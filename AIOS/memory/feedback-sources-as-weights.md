Murphy regards the early source documents (e.g. the Catechism and Confession, written before the Number Hierarchy) as drafts that "can be wrong" and as weights holding back new writing, not as authorities to preserve.

**Why:** canonical pages supersede the drafts; fidelity to a superseded draft blocks new work.

**How to apply:** where a snapshot and a canonical page differ, the page governs. Support deletion of a source once an absorption check confirms its content lives on canonical pages — decided for the Catechism and Confession (2026-11-06, Future Directions item 11): they will not be finished or unified; after the absorption check, delete both snapshots and clean the links (Home, `sources:` lines, Three Tiers, Geometry Hierarchy, Creaturely Realism, Timeline of the Position).
