Murphy: "I haven't actually made that year. You can list, and mark it as future pedagogical work." The K-12 proof-type grade bands came from a planning draft; they are a plan.

**How to apply:** on any page that gives grade bands, a sequence, a rubric or a unit plan that has not been used in a classroom, say so plainly and collect the items under a "Future pedagogical work" heading (see `Pedagogy/Quadriga for Mathematics`). Do not write them as the vault's curriculum.
