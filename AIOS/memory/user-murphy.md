Robert Marshall Murphy: mathematics educator and author of an original mathematical position (creaturely realism, certificate-explicit constructivism, creational ultrafinitism), grounded in a Reformed theology of creatures before an infinite God. Builds the "My Math" vault to gather all of it in one place before any book is written. Also the author of the Quadriga Scientiae ([[project-quadriga]]).

**How he works:** talks through an idea, then says "do it". Corrects misreadings directly and expects them taken on board ([[feedback-faithful-recording]]). Wants hard problems flagged, not smoothed over, and no unnecessary follow-up questions at the end of a reply. Likes real worked numbers that have been checked. Reads Kline, Wildberger, Bell, Lockhart, Pólya. Uses the Latin/Greek names of his frameworks.
