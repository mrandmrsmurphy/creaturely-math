"This isn't a science vault, so skip that." When documenting a framework that also has science, school or lab applications (the Quadriga did), include only the mathematical content, state in one line that the rest is outside the vault, and keep a single principle that matters (Scale: the creature in the middle).

Also: the vault is "my first time trying to tie all my math thoughts together", and separate projects must be **tied in**, not just filed (see [[project-quadriga]]: `Foundations/Quadriga and the Position`).

**How to apply:** before importing a body of material, ask what part is mathematics; map it to the three-part position (what mathematics is, how we know it, how big it is) and link it from `Home`, `Glossary`, `Timeline of the Position` and the page it relates to. Documentation, not book drafting (`Meta/Future Directions` guideline 3).
